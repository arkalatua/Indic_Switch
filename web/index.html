    Four steps. The raw output is always kept.

    1. 01

      Audio, in your browser

      Any file or recording is converted on this page to 16 kHz mono before it is sent. Nothing is saved on the server.

    2. 02

      Whisper writes it down

      OpenAI’s Whisper transcribes the clip. Blocking Urdu script keeps Hindi words in Devanagari.

    3. 03

      Every word gets a language

      A tagger trained on 23,698 annotated Hinglish sentences labels each word Hindi, English or other, with a confidence.

    4. 04

      Careful fixes, all logged

      Only confident words change: Whisper’s repeated loops, Hindi spelling variants, English words written in Devanagari.

    Limits worth knowing. Measured, not guessed.

    • Fixes cannot hear

      The fixes clean what Whisper wrote. A word Whisper misheard stays misheard.

    • Base is weak on fast speech

      On conversational clips Whisper Base often loops or drifts into Urdu script. Small does better and is slower.

    • Loops are the big win

      Removing repeated loops took word error rate from 1.52 to 0.99 on 20 test clips, with no clip made worse.

    • Ambiguous words

      “me” and “to” are both Hindi and English words, and cause most tagging mistakes.
